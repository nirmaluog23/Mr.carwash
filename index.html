Questions people ask

What do I need to provide?

Just 2 buckets of water and one electric plug point near the car. We bring the shampoo, cloths, vacuum and machine.

Do I have to be at home during the wash?

We need the car keys for the interior clean, so someone at home or a guard who can hand them over is enough.

How long does it take?

About an hour for a full interior and exterior wash.

How do I pay?

After you send the booking, we share our UPI ID or QR code on WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received, and we then send your wash time and your car washer's details.

How early do I need to book?

At least 2 hours before your slot. The booking form only shows slots that are still open.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp at least 2 hours before your slot and we'll move it.
